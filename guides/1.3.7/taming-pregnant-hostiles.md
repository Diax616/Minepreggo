# Taming Pregnant Preggo Mobs

Some preggo mobs spawn already pregnant, at stage **P3, P5 or P7**. You can tame them with
the right food. This doc covers how to tame each one.

For what happens once the babies arrive, see [Babies](babies.md).

---

## How taming works

The process is the same for every species. Only the food, the number of feedings and the
conditions change.

**1. Build up affection.** Right-click her with her taming food. Each feeding uses up one
item, adds affection and puffs out a little smoke. Keep going until she's had enough.

**2. Win her over.** Once her affection is full, each further feeding is a roll to tame her.
These rolls **don't use up the food**. Hearts mean it worked. Smoke means it didn't, so try
again.

Affection is remembered. If you walk away halfway through, she still remembers how much she's
eaten when you come back.

Pregnant preggo mobs **don't accept the usual taming food**. They only want their **craving
foods**, the same seasoned dishes they crave during pregnancy. Any flavor works: sweet, salty,
sour or spicy.

---

## Zombie Girl

| | |
|---|---|
| **Food** | Brain With Chocolate, Brain With Hot Sauce, Brain With Salt, Sour Brain |
| **Feedings needed** | 5 |
| **Chance per roll** | 50% in a cave, guaranteed anywhere else |
| **Refuses while** | She's on fire |

While she's burning, nothing happens when you feed her. Wait for the fire to go out.

---

## Creeper Girl

Applies to both the **Humanoid** and the **Monster** Creeper Girl.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Food** | Activated Gunpowder With Chocolate, Activated Gunpowder With Hot Sauce, Activated Gunpowder With Salt, Sour Activated Gunpowder |
| **Feedings needed** | 7 |
| **Chance per roll** | 50% in a cave, guaranteed anywhere else |
| **Refuses while** | She's swelling up or has been ignited |

A creeper girl that's about to explode won't eat anything. Back off and wait for her to calm
down.

---

## Ender Woman

Applies to the **Monster** Ender Woman.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Food** | Ender Slime Jelly With Chocolate, Ender Slime Jelly With Hot Sauce, Ender Slime Jelly With Salt, Sour Ender Slime Jelly |
| **Feedings needed** | 10 |
| **Chance per roll** | 10% in the End, guaranteed anywhere else |
| **Refuses while** | She's provoked or attacking |

If you've angered her, by looking at her or hitting her, nothing happens when you feed her. Let
her calm down first. Taming her outside the End is much easier.

---

## Slime Girl

Applies to both the **Humanoid** and the **Monster** Slime Girl.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Food** | Rotten Flesh With Chocolate, Rotten Flesh With Hot Sauce, Rotten Flesh With Salt, Sour Rotten Flesh, Spider Eye With Chocolate, Spider Eye With Hot Sauce, Spider Eye With Salt, Sour Spider Eye |
| **Feedings needed** | 12 |
| **Chance per roll** | Always guaranteed |
| **Refuses while** | She holds a grudge against you |

### The grudge

Slime girls stick together. If you **kill a slime girl**, every other slime girl within
**32 blocks** holds a grudge against you for **one in-game day**. Until it wears off she won't
take food from you at all. You get a chat message saying so, and she shows angry particles.

The grudge is only against the player who did the killing. A friend can still tame her.

## Quick reference

| Species | Food | Feedings | Harder when | Refuses while |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zombie Girl | Seasoned brains | 5 | In a cave (50%) | On fire |
| Creeper Girl | Seasoned activated gunpowder | 7 | In a cave (50%) | Swelling or ignited |
| Ender Woman | Seasoned ender slime jelly | 10 | In the End (10%) | Provoked or attacking |
| Slime Girl | Seasoned rotten flesh or spider eye | 12 | Never | Holding a grudge |

- Feed until her affection is full, then keep feeding. Each roll is free, and hearts mean
  success.
- The usual taming food doesn't work on a pregnant preggo mob. Only her craving foods do.
