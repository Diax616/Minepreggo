# Fatigue

Carrying a baby around is exhausting. Once you hit **P3**, running, jumping and crouching all
start wearing you out. Push it too far and you'll get hit with **Fatigue**: you hit softer,
swing slower, move slower, and your vision starts acting up.

The good news: it's easy to fix. Lie down and get some rest.

> **Note:** Tamed pregnant mob girls get tired too, they just get there by walking around.
> See [Tamed mob girls](#tamed-mob-girls).

---

## Your energy bar

From P3 on, there's an extra bar in the top-left corner of your screen with the Fatigue icon.
That's your **energy**. It starts full (20) and drains as you get tired.

- Full bar: you're fresh.
- Getting low: you're running on fumes, and Fatigue can kick in any time.
- Empty: you're completely wiped out. You can't get any more tired than this, but Fatigue
  hits its hardest.

Your energy **doesn't come back on its own**. Taking it easy stops it from draining, but the
only way to fill it back up is to [rest](#resting).

---

## What tires you out

Each of these drains your energy a little at a time. The further along you are, the faster it
happens.

| Activity | Starts at | How fast it drains you |
|---|---|---|
| Sprinting | P3 | Fastest |
| Jumping | P3 | Pretty fast |
| Crouching | P4 | Medium |

Walking around normally is fine. It's sprinting, bunny-hopping and sneaking everywhere that
add up.

Late in the pregnancy, the same activity drains you a whole lot faster. At P3 you can sprint
for a good while before it costs you anything. By P10 it takes a fraction of that.

---

## Getting Fatigue

Once your energy drops far enough (**14 or lower**), you might come down with Fatigue at any
moment. It's random, so you won't always get it right away, but it gets more likely in the
later phases.

How bad it hits depends on how drained you are when it starts:

| Energy left | You get |
|---|---|
| 15 to 20 | Nothing, you're fine |
| 9 to 14 | **Fatigue I** |
| 1 to 8 | **Fatigue II** |
| 0 (empty) | **Fatigue III** |

Fatigue lasts a few minutes: about 2 at P3, up to around 5 near the end.

**Milk won't cure it.** You have to wait it out or go rest.

---

## What Fatigue does

| Level | Effects |
|---|---|
| **Fatigue I** | You deal 20% less damage |
| **Fatigue II** | All of the above, plus you swing 10% slower. Your vision starts going (see below) |
| **Fatigue III** | All of the above, plus you move 10% slower |

### Your vision

From **Fatigue II** up, being this tired messes with your eyes:

- **Colors fade.** The world slowly turns grayish while you have it. It gets a bit grayer at
  Fatigue III.
- **Every 30 seconds or so**, you'll get a few seconds where your vision goes weird. It's one
  of these, picked at random:
  - **Double vision:** you see a blurry second copy of everything, drifting around.
  - **Wobble:** the whole screen sways and ripples, like you're dizzy.

  It fades in, sticks around for about 8 seconds, then fades back out.

All of this goes away as soon as the Fatigue effect wears off.

---

## Resting

> **The only way to get rid of your tiredness is resting in a bed.** Just standing still or
> taking it easy won't do it.

To get your energy back, **lie down in a bed**. Every 10 to 20 seconds you stay there, you
get back a good chunk of energy (5 points on the bar). The further along you are, the longer
each chunk takes.

You'll see happy green sparkles around you while you're recovering.

If you rest until your energy bar is completely full, your Fatigue goes away too, even if it
hadn't worn off yet.

---

## Tamed mob girls

Tamed pregnant mob girls get tired too, but a little differently:

- **Walking** is what wears them out, starting at P3. Riding something doesn't count.
- They get the same **Fatigue I to III** levels and the same weaker hits and slower swings and
  movement as you do.
- They rest by **sleeping**, and get their energy back the same way.
- You can check how tired they are on the energy bar in their menu.

The vision effects are only for you, since they're about what's on your screen.

---

## Quick tips

- Don't sprint everywhere late in the pregnancy. Walk when you can.
- Stop spam-jumping. It adds up faster than you'd think.
- Keep an eye on the energy bar. Once it's down to 14 or lower, you're rolling the dice every moment.
- Planning a fight? Get some rest first. Fatigue cuts your damage right from level I.
- Fatigue is a lot more annoying with weird vision. If your screen starts going gray, it's
  time for a nap.
